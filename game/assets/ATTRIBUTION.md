# Ledger assets

- `ledger_background.png`: generated specifically for Underworld's approved
  Family Ledger concept. It is a blank paper/leather background; gameplay
  information is rendered dynamically.
- `fonts/LiberationSerif-Regular.ttf`, `fonts/LiberationSerif-Bold.ttf`, and
  `fonts/LiberationMono-Regular.ttf`: unmodified Liberation fonts, distributed
  under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Copyright notices and the license are
  included in `fonts/LICENSE.txt`. Upstream: https://github.com/liberationfonts.
- `fonts/Silkscreen-Regular.ttf`: Silkscreen by Jason Kottke, distributed under
  the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The unmodified font and its notices are retained
  in `fonts/Silkscreen-OFL.txt`. Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/silkscreen.
- The mobile notebook binding, button textures, member portrait patterns, city
  blocks, and `underworld_icon.svg` are small procedural or authored game UI
  assets. Approved generated concept sheets remain in `docs/concepts/` as design
  references and are excluded from mobile exports.
