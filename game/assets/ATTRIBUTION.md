# Ledger assets

- `ledger_background.png`: generated specifically for Underworld's approved
  Family Ledger concept. It is a blank paper/leather background; gameplay
  information is rendered dynamically.
- `fonts/LiberationSerif-Regular.ttf`, `fonts/LiberationSerif-Bold.ttf`, and
  `fonts/LiberationMono-Regular.ttf`: unmodified Liberation fonts, distributed
  under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Copyright notices and the license are
  included in `fonts/LICENSE.txt`. Upstream: https://github.com/liberationfonts.
